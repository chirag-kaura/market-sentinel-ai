# Phase 08 — Step 04 Report: Standardized Alert Schemas & Persistence Models

## 1. Objective & Business Relevance
Regulatory surveillance mandates that every alert generated by an algorithmic system must be immutable, auditable, and traceable to its raw market data snapshot, news text citations, and mathematical model weights. Ad-hoc logging or ephemeral alerts undermine regulatory defensibility during SEBI or FINRA audits.

**Step 8.4** establishes `SurveillanceAlertSchema` (Pydantic v2 validation) and `SurveillanceAlertModel` (SQLAlchemy ORM mapped to PostgreSQL `surveillance_alerts`), providing strongly typed schema enforcement and relational persistence.

---

## 2. Technical Implementation Details
- **Module**: `intelligence/correlation/alert_models.py`
  - `SurveillanceAlertSchema`: Pydantic schema validating `alert_id`, `ticker`, `company_name`, `alert_timestamp`, `priority_tier`, `composite_risk_score` $[0.0, 1.0]$, `confidence` $[0.0, 1.0]$, `primary_risk_driver`, `temporal_relationship`, `sla_triage_hours`, `requires_human_escalation`, `recommended_action`, and nested audit dictionaries (`market_snapshot`, `news_snapshot`, `risk_breakdown`).
  - `SurveillanceAlertModel`: SQLAlchemy ORM mapping with database indexes on `alert_id`, `ticker`, `alert_timestamp`, `priority_tier`, and composite index on `(ticker, alert_timestamp)`. Uses cross-dialect `JSON` column types compatible with both PostgreSQL `JSONB` and SQLite test fixtures.
